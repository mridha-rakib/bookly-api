# Central Withdrawal reserve decision

> **Update 17 November 2026:** the Owner confirmed the ownership and manual-settlement rules (A, B, H; no second approval, I). They are implemented as policy CW-1 in `CENTRAL-WITHDRAWAL-ELIGIBILITY.md`, which replaces the hard-coded source denial with eligibility, reservation and LIVE-flag controls. Category J (Stripe, legal and accountant confirmation) remains external. LIVE stays off by deployment flag until a release is authorized. The original analysis below is kept for reference.

Status (original): **OWNER/ACCOUNTING DECISION PENDING**. Date: 8 November 2026.

LIVE Bookly payouts remain source-denied, including when the deployment flag is enabled. A bank account visible in Stripe, a verified bank, a positive Stripe balance, and Super Admin access do not constitute reserve approval. No numerical policy is proposed here.

| Required decision | Current code support | Owner / specialist decision | Work required after approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Platform-owned funds | Existing ledger ownership helpers distinguish Bookly revenue, Business inflows, processing fees, refunds and dispute effects. Provider available balance is a separate read. | Accountant/Owner must define when each source is earned, settled, attributable and withdrawable, including historical/unassigned amounts. | Compose an authoritative per-currency eligibility calculation using existing primitives; fail closed on incomplete ownership/reconciliation. Do not equate the provider pool with revenue. |
| B. Business/customer liabilities | Business payable reads and booking/payment/refund records provide inputs. They are not a complete withdrawal reserve. | Identify all outstanding Business settlements, deposits, customer entitlements, cancellation/refund obligations and unsettled operations requiring protection. | Reconcile the complete obligation set with the provider account and preserve it atomically against withdrawals and competing financial operations. |
| C. Refund/dispute exposure | Refund and provider-reversal domains track concrete effects; central overview does not calculate prospective exposure. | Accountant/provider/Owner must define funded liabilities versus future exposure, protections, dispute-fee treatment and release evidence. | Integrate approved exposure inputs without double counting ledger effects or releasing funds on an uncertain result. |
| D. Tax/fee exposure | Existing financial ownership/fee records are available; Business fee-VAT responsibility is separately unresolved. | Accountant/legal must decide platform tax liabilities, Business tax responsibility, provider/bank fees, and what must remain funded. | Add approved inputs and failure states; never infer exemption or invent tax amounts from missing configuration. |
| E. Pending payout reservations | Central operation IDs, original provider idempotency keys, correlation, active dispatch slot and UNKNOWN recovery already exist. Recent provider pending totals are a bounded informational sample, not a complete reservation ledger. | Decide how Bookly-created, Dashboard-created and automatic payouts affect available eligibility and when reservations may be released. | Implement race-safe eligibility reservation, provider dispatch and reconciliation using the existing operation/ledger architecture. Account for ambiguous results and prevent another identity from spending reserved funds. |
| F. Per-currency treatment | Provider balances and destinations are currency-specific; current Business ledger reporting is EUR. | Approve supported settlement currencies, ownership mapping, any conversions and who bears conversion costs. | Calculate and reserve each currency separately. Do not offset one currency's obligations using an invented conversion or another currency's balance. |
| G. Reserve release | Provider lifecycle and audit history exist. There is no approved reserve release engine. | Define release conditions, evidence, responsible reviewer and response to new liability/dispute/provider failure. Specify any time rules only after these obligations are identified. | Implement auditable release transitions and concurrency controls. Age, a missing search result or an estimated arrival date cannot prove settlement/release. |
| H. Authorizer | Real Super Admin role gate and current-password step-up exist. | Owner must identify which financial administrators may authorize which withdrawals and how access revocation affects pending requests. | Implement any narrower financial authority and freshness/revalidation requirements; roles alone cannot grant reserve approval. |
| I. Second approval | No two-person approval workflow exists. | Owner/accountant must decide whether independent approval is required and its scope, separation of duties, expiry and rejection behavior. | If required, bind approval to the immutable amount/currency/destination/eligibility snapshot and prevent self-approval or stale execution. No arbitrary threshold is supplied. |
| J. Provider/legal/accounting confirmation | Stripe is the platform payout provider; current adapter reads the key's account and exposes masked fields only. | Confirm the legal entity/account, bank ownership, supported key permissions, schedule, payout constraints, customer/Business money obligations, tax position and external/automatic payout controls. | Qualify the real account separately and document operational reconciliation, error/recovery and authorized release procedures. |

The Owner must approve a complete obligation-backed policy rather than an arbitrary reserve percentage or buffer. Continued LIVE denial is the currently supported safe production choice. Fixed-buffer or time/exposure-hold designs would still require the ownership/liability controls above; neither is a selectable implemented reserve policy.

Approval of this document would not itself activate payouts. Implementation and mocked regression coverage, accounting/provider qualification, review of reservation/concurrency behavior, and a separately approved source/deployment release are required. The existing environment flag cannot bypass the source denial. Existing Stripe fees/contracts still apply; no service or subscription is introduced.
